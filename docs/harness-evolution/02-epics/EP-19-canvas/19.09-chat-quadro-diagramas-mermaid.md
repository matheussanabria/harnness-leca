# EP-19.09: Board chat: Mermaid diagrams on the Excalidraw board

## Objective
Let the Studio chat draw on the free board: the answer carries a `<board mode="replace|append">` block with a Mermaid diagram.

## Requirements
- The legacy bridge supports a second kind (`board`) with its own rules; each kind ignores the other's state and answers.
- Pure helpers: summary of the drawing for the prompt, validation of the block (flowchart/graph, sequenceDiagram, classDiagram only), placement below existing content.
- The island converts with `@excalidraw/mermaid-to-excalidraw` (lazy, direct pinned dependency) and applies the scene as one undoable Excalidraw step; refused or invalid blocks show a short note.

## Tests
Pure helpers under `node:test`; browser check with a real flowchart in both modes and undo.
